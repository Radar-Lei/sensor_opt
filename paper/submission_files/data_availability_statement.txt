Data Availability Statement
===========================

The TRACE-BiOpt code, experiment scripts, generated tables, and paper-facing
evidence artifacts are available in the project repository.

The repository includes:
- the TRACE-BiOpt implementation and experiment launch scripts;
- curated aggregate result artifacts under `TRC-23-02333/trace_sl_results/`;
- the paper source and submission-facing tables/figures; and
- documentation for reproducing the reported results from the curated
  artifacts.

Raw PeMS and Seattle traffic datasets are not redistributed with the
repository. Reproduction therefore requires obtaining the underlying traffic
datasets separately and placing them in the local directory structure expected
by the experiment scripts. The repository documents the required local data
paths and the commands used to regenerate the reported evidence.

Raw per-seed outputs are not all bundled into the paper package because of
their size, but the curated result summaries and provenance artifacts used for
the manuscript are included.
